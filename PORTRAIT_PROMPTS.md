# Crew Portrait Prompts

These keep every portrait in the same style as `bill.jpeg` (day, dock and speedboat) and `mario.jpeg` (night, neon hotels and muscle car).

## How to use
1. Upload the person's reference photo (a clear, front-facing shot of their face).
2. Paste the **Base style** text, then add **one scene** from the list below.
3. Save the result as `images/crew/{slug}.png` or `.jpeg`, where `{slug}` is the person's lowercase first name, e.g. `alex.jpeg`.

**Specs:** portrait 4:5, about 928×1152 or larger. The full body or a three-quarter view is fine, but **the face must sit in the top third** (the site's name caption covers the bottom of the card). Use one person per image.

---

## Base style (paste first, every time)

> Illustrated portrait in the style of modern open-world crime video game loading-screen art: bold clean black ink outlines, cel-shaded comic-book coloring, slightly exaggerated heroic proportions, detailed painted background. Make the subject look like the person in the reference photo: keep their face, hair and build recognizable. Show them standing or posed confidently near the center of the frame, facing mostly toward the viewer, with the face clearly visible in the upper third. Set the scene in Miami, Florida, with palm trees, and add a small police or news helicopter in the sky as a recurring detail. Vertical 4:5 composition. No readable text, no logos, no watermarks, no weapons.

Day scenes: add **"warm golden-hour daylight, hazy turquoise and cream palette."**
Night scenes: add **"night, neon pink, purple and cyan lighting, sunset-violet sky."**

### Tips for consistency
- If a result drifts from the style, add: *"match the art style exactly: thick black outlines, flat cel shading, no photorealism."*
- To mix days and nights evenly, alternate them as you assign scenes. The grid looks best with a roughly 50/50 mix of warm day and neon night images.
- Try to give each person something to hold (a drink, cigar, bag or buoy). It makes the portrait feel like a character card.
- Already used: **Bill** for dock and speedboat (day) and **Mario** for neon hotels and muscle car (night). Avoid giving those two scenes to anyone else.
